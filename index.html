About Me

Carlo Cordero

Photographer & Video Editor

I have been working in photography and video editing for approximately four years. My interest in photography began through a friend who was already involved in the craft — that early exposure sparked a curiosity that grew into a discipline, and eventually into a creative style of my own.

My goal is simple: provide clients with quality creative output that genuinely satisfies what they set out to capture, while continuing to build honest, lasting relationships with the people he works with.
